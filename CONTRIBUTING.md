# Contributing

Bug reports and feature ideas are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/AlvaroMunozS/AniHub/issues/new/choose). Use the
templates and search the existing issues first.

Pull requests from outside contributors are not accepted: AniHub is maintained
by one person and kept deliberately small. To propose a change, open an issue
that describes the problem.

Security issues are reported privately, as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

AniHub tracks which anime you are watching, plan to watch and have completed.
Requests are measured against that. The following are out of scope:

- Statuses beyond *watching*, *planned* and *completed*.
- Scores, reviews, tags, notes or statistics.
- Episode counters, progress tracking or watch dates.
- Accounts, sync servers or social features.
- Importing lists from MyAnimeList or other trackers.

Requests that fall outside the scope are closed with the `out of scope` label.
